How to Make $1 Million from Blogging (from a blogger)!

Patrick Rowe | 7/26/2026

If you are reading this, you are definitely unemployed, and *most likely* a completely uninteresting person. Did you even leave the house today? Did you make your bed? Have you done something unexpected in the last month?

If the answer to all of these is no, there is still hope in becoming an internet sensation. All you have to do is start a blog!

Starting a Blog

The first thing you have to do to start a blog is to make a website. For this, I would go to GitHub, make an account, and create a repository. From there, you can ask AI how to make a website.

After you make a website, you have to fill it with content. This can be text, audio, video, or a combination of these that entertains people. Think about Key and Peele making funny skits! You could easily do the same; nothing is stopping you!

Running and Managing a Blog

Once you find your content groove, you need to start posting consistently. I recommend posting once every three or four days as a beginner. This frequency will satisfy your viewers and give you time to do the real work: monetizing your blog.

You will set up a monetization machine in the background of your blog, invisible to viewers, that makes you a millionaire!

Make your Money

How do you set up this machine? Well, to paraphrase Graham Cochrane, "Once you give enough value to society, you will be reimbursed for it."

In other words, you have to get off your ass and stop being a lazy bum. Get a job you fat piece of shit!

The Reality

There is no such thing as get-rich-quick. You've somehow deluded yourself into chasing a unicorn... from your basement. Touch grass buddy.
